Privacy Policy — Dancing Dead Uploader

Last updated: July 4, 2026

1. Whose data is processed

Only data related to TikTok accounts owned by Dancing Dead Records. The tool is internal: no data about members of the public is collected.

2. What is stored

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the label's TikTok accounts, stored on infrastructure controlled by the label.
  • Basic profile information of those accounts (display name, open id) returned by the TikTok API.
  • The label's own videos and their publishing schedule.

3. What is NOT done

  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any user.
  • No data of TikTok users other than the label's own accounts is accessed or stored.

4. Data retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when an account is disconnected. Revoking the app's access from TikTok's settings immediately invalidates them.
